# Business Overview

## What this business does

This is a broad consumer ecommerce business selling products across 18 categories, including accessories, skincare, haircare, shoes, home decor, kitchen products, smartwatches, speakers, jackets, makeup, jeans, headphones and bedding. The catalog contains 4,000 products. Four additional categories like Electronics, Apparel, Beauty and Home these currently contain no products. All products are assigned to a category.

## Scale

The database contains 10,000 customers and 40,000 orders, covering activity from March 16 to June 14, 2026. That works out to an overall average of 4 orders per customer. All orders are linked to an existing customer, so we found no orphaned orders in this check. The relatively short three month observation window means these figures should be interpreted as activity during this period rather than as a full-year view of the business.

## What I noticed

Customer purchasing is highly uneven. Of the 10,000 customers, 1,200 (12%) have not placed an order, 4,999 (50%) have placed exactly one order, and 3,801 (38%) are repeat buyers. While the overall average is 4 orders per customer, repeat buyers average about 9.2 orders each. This suggests that a relatively small group of engaged customers generates a large share of repeat purchasing, while a substantial portion of the customer base has either never purchased or purchased only once. This customer split is worth investigating further to understand retention and opportunities to convert one-time buyers into repeat customers.
